## Description (150 words)

> HORDES is a free pixel-art survivor game you can play hands-on or leave on autopilot. Your hero fights on their own; steer when you want to. Every level you draft a card, and your cards form a poker hand: rank is rarity, suit is family, and pairs, straights, flushes and straight flushes add bonuses. Jokers bend the rules. Weapons evolve at level 8, and evolved pairs fuse.
> Beat each wave's boss and take the portal; a short chase scene banks your escape gold. After waves 2 and 4 the portal leads to a new stage, with shrines, altars, a locked vault and secrets, plus three quests every run.
> Your first run is short: the horde wins, and the gold you bank buys permanent upgrades in a 42-row shop. Four characters, auto-continue and a camp that earns while you are away. Recorded music, and it keeps working offline once loaded. A short guided run teaches the basics.

## Update note (for the page's news or changelog field)

> November update:
> - The level-up cards wait for you: AUTO-PICK ON/OFF and a WAIT switch (6 s down to 1 s) are on the draft screen.
> - Straight Flush is a hand now.
> - Recorded music: a title theme, a track for each group of stages, the boss, the Maw and the escape. The old chiptune is still there under Settings > ADVANCED.
> - The autopilot moves smoothly: no more jitter between enemies, and it keeps off the walls.
> - Enemy shots are red-orange orbs, so they no longer look like gems.
> - Auto-continue pays in full for the first three runs in a row, then 75%.
> - The game keeps a copy of itself in your browser and tells you when a new version is ready.
> - Sound effects have a new mix: louder, softer hits, less harsh.
> - Auto-continue is on the end card, where you look for it.
> - Coming back after a break no longer hides your first draft.
> - Weapons arrive during the run: your loadout lists the weapons a run can offer, you start with one, and the rest come as NEW cards in your drafts. The first one is a free pick.

## Replies to the three October comments

**"How do I stop the auto-purchase timer? I want to read the upgrades but I only have 5 seconds"**

> Fixed. The draft screen now has two switches: AUTO-PICK ON/OFF (key U) and WAIT, which goes from 6 seconds down to 1 (key T). Turn AUTO-PICK off and the cards wait until you choose. Both are remembered. Thanks for saying it plainly: it was the top complaint.

**"Is there no straight flush? I have 23456 of spades and it still only has spade flush"**

> There is now. A Straight Flush ranks above Four of a Kind: it pays Four's bonus, the Straight's faster fire and the suit's own bonus. Your 2-3-4-5-6 of spades counts.

**"I don't mind AI usage... I DO mind it where AI isn't strong enough yet: the synthesized music and sounds"**

> Fair. The music is recorded tracks now (a title theme, four stage groups, the boss, the Maw, the escape), made with Google's Lyria and picked by ear, take by take. The old synth is still there as an option if anyone misses it. The sound effects have a new mix too: louder, softer hits, less harsh.

## Optional reply to the older "I have to babysit it" comments (grotus, potthole)

> Auto-continue pays in full for the first three runs in a row now (then 75%, it was 50% from the first), and it is on the end card now instead of deep in Settings.
