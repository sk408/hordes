# Player review — 2026-09-17

Captured verbatim from the owner (relayed from a player). This is the reference document for the
"condense and polish" round. Nothing here is assigned yet; the mechanical items are listed at the bottom.

## Verbatim

The core gameplay is actually pretty good, much better than the ratings suggest. However, there's A LOT to
polish. Off the top of my head:

- The tutorial. Enough has been said already. My main caveat is that clicking "skip" still shows you the next
  chips.
- Weird line between idle and active. The fact that runs don't auto-restart (nor waves) means I can't actually
  let the game idle for hours and expect to come up with at least one or two upgrades the following night.
- Progression is irregular. Once you get the second weapon (and find where to equip it), you quickly start
  getting lots of gold per run. But once you're at the fourth weapon and have bought all the "cheap" upgrades,
  a "good" run nets you only 1/4th of a single upgrade. Given the point above, it means babysitting the game
  to reset runs which quickly gets boring.
- The game gives the illusion of multiple paths & playstyles but the actual balancing negates this. Mana regen
  is overpowered, so you never need the cap, and every character type becomes the same. On the flip side,
  health drops (and regains) so rapidly that only your max matters. Potions are illusory - there's always
  enough on the ground and it's not clear how they work.
- WAAY too many settings. Different arenas are barely noticeable, special (no potion, etc) runs don't clearly
  explain what you GET from doing them.
- The minigame is a nice idea but, for me, just breaks the experience. Too long, not enough payout means I
  always click skip.

All in all, I think the game tries to do too much (too many concepts, characters, weapons, upgrades, etc)
without really motivating you along the way. I don't know what I should be looking forward to, the next step
feels too far away and so I end up closing the tab. But I had fun along the way, the animations are cool and I
love seeing lightning zap all those chasers :)

I honestly think you're on to something pretty good here, but you need to sit down and think about what you
want. Condense, keep the good stuff, drop complexity and you'll end up with something special.

## Extraction

MECHANICAL / CLARITY (assigned, round 1):
1. Skipping the tutorial still shows the following chips - a defect, not a design question.
2. Special runs (no-potion etc.) do not state what the player GETS for the restriction. Say the reward up
   front, before the run starts.
3. Potions: it is not clear how they work. The manual must explain pickup, capacity, effect and refill, and
   the HP/MP controls should read as consumables.
4. Minigame: too long, payout too small relative to the time it costs. Shorten the interaction and raise the
   payout, or make declining it cost nothing.

DESIGN CALLS (owner's, not the lane's):
A. Auto-restart / idle behaviour: runs and waves do not advance themselves, so the game cannot be left
   running. Decide whether HORDES supports an opt-in auto/idle mode.
B. Progression curve: gold income per run versus upgrade cost drifts badly at the fourth weapon and beyond;
   "a good run nets a quarter of one upgrade".
C. Path differentiation: mana regen is strong enough that the cap never matters and characters converge;
   health swings so fast that only max HP matters. The resources need to differ in KIND, not quantity.
D. Settings surface: too many options, arenas barely distinguishable, run modifiers under-explained.
E. The headline: the game carries too many concepts without a clear "what am I working towards".
