HORDES sound check

What to listen for (about 2 minutes, with headphones or speakers at your usual level):
  1. Press PLAY A BUSY FIGHT on NEW, then on OLD. The new one should be louder, the hits less hissy, and no crackle.
  2. Press BUSY FIGHT + MUSIC. The fight should sit with the music, not drown it.
  3. Press Boss roar and Escape roar under Movie cues. Loud, but no longer a jump scare next to the fight.
  4. Press chest, then chestEmpty. The empty one should feel like a let-down.
  5. Press gem, then gemRich. The rich gem (many gems merged into one) should feel like a payoff.
  6. At the bottom, under Recorded sounds: pick, choose a recorded sound for each slot (or keep the synth) and send the line it gives you.
  7. Under Sounds that mean something, play the eight hands from Pair to Straight Flush. Each should be bigger than the one before, not just louder. Then the heartbeat at 25%, 15% and 5%: soft, and faster as health falls.

The NEW / OLD switch is saved in this browser, and the game on the same site uses it too: pick OLD to turn the new mix off in the game. Numbers beside each sound: its loudest moment in LUFS, new and old (higher is louder; 10 more is about twice as loud to the ear).

Recorded sounds: pick

Recorded sound effects (free CC0 recordings) could replace the synth ones. For each sound below:
  1. Under Alone, press Synth today, then each letter. Hits, kills and gems play their 3 or 4 variations in a row.
  2. Under In a fight (or In the menus), press each one: the same busy fight with only that sound changed. This is what counts.
  3. Under My pick, choose the one you like best, or Keep synth if none fits.
  4. At the bottom, press COPY and send that line to Claude.

Your picks are saved in this browser. In the game, Settings, ADVANCED, SOUND EFFECTS: RECORDED (PREVIEW) plays the A of each sound until your picks are in.
