# HORDES — October 2026 update

**Play it:** https://sk408.github.io/hordes/ · the previous version stays playable at https://sk408.github.io/hordes/classic/

## Your save
- Shop upgrades from before this update are refunded in gold at the price you paid; the shop is rebuilt and repriced. Weapons, characters and elite unlocks stay owned.
- Your save from before the update is kept untouched. Download it from Settings, or keep playing it in the classic version, which has its own save from now on.

## New
- **Evolutions:** a weapon at level 8 evolves when you hold its partner card. Every weapon has one, and the cards show the recipe.
- **Fusions:** two evolved weapons can fuse into one new weapon and free a slot.
- **Hands:** the cards you draft form a poker hand; better hands pay a run-long bonus.
- **Jokers:** one row of rule-changing cards, offered in drafts and by every wave boss.
- **Reroll, skip and banish** in the draft.
- **A guided first run** that teaches by doing, with first-time hints after it. Replay it from Settings.

## Changed
- Progress is steadier: each run should last a little longer and buy about one upgrade.
- Owned weapons are equipped by default. Every Vitality level now matters. Potions heal a share of max health.
- Clearer enemies on brighter ground, damage numbers, hit and death effects, larger sprites.
- New sounds per weapon, a boss music section, and volume sliders.
- A simpler title and settings screen; keyboard hints replace the touch pads on desktop.
- The autopilot finds its way around buildings.
- The escape sequence is shorter and shows its payout up front.

## 1.0 (September 2026)

**Play it:** https://sk408.github.io/hordes/

HORDES is a pixel-art survivor game about managing a squeeze. Waves pour in and keep getting heavier, and
staying alive is all movement: cutting through gaps, dragging a horde behind you, making a shrinking arena
work in your favour. Level up and you draft a card. Kill the boss and a portal opens. It is the same fight
every time and it keeps rewarding you for learning it.

Your build is a deck of playing cards: rank is rarity, suit is family, the combos are the face cards, and the
jokers are the ones everybody chases. You pick your weapons before the run, and you have to go find them. Turn
up the heat and the rewards get richer. Take the portal and the game flips side-on for the escape, a minute of
running for your life with three pursuers minimum at your heels matching your speed, every pit a chance to
watch them drop. Four characters play it differently, a 48-item shop gives you a long climb, and death always
pays, because what you bank becomes the upgrades that make the next run meaner.
